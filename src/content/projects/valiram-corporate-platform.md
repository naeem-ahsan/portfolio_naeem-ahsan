## Overview

The Valiram Corporate Platform is a custom WordPress website developed to support Valiram’s multi-brand business structure across different markets and content needs. The platform uses reusable theme components, custom post types, structured content, dynamic templates and custom search functionality to make it easier for internal teams to manage brands, stores, campaigns, careers, and corporate information from a central CMS.

## Challenge

The platform needed to accommodate a growing portfolio of brands, country-specific content, store information, career opportunities, and frequently updated corporate content without relying on rigid page-by-page development.

The main challenge was creating a flexible content structure that could support different brand requirements while maintaining consistent design, performance, SEO, and ease of use for content administrators. As well as the existing search experience needed to handle a wide range of content types and return more relevant results without forcing full page reloads.

## Approach

- Developed custom WordPress theme components and plugins based on the platform’s business requirements.
- Created reusable templates and content sections to reduce duplicated development across pages.
- Structured brand, store, career, and related content using custom post types, taxonomies, and custom fields.
- Implemented dynamic brand pages and category-based content relationships.
- Built country-specific and brand-specific store directory experiences.
- Developed reusable sliders, shortcodes, filters, and content modules that could be managed through the CMS.
- Added fallback handling for missing images, links, logos, and optional content.
- Improved responsive behaviour, frontend performance, SEO implementation, and content maintainability.
- Supported ongoing campaign updates, UI enhancements, bug fixes, and evolving business requirements.
- Developed a custom AJAX-powered search experience to improve speed, usability, and result relevance.
- Added support for searching across multiple content types, including pages, posts, brands, jobs, and other structured content.
- Improved search-result filtering, keyword matching, and frontend responsiveness without requiring full page reloads.

## Contributions

- Led the end-to-end WordPress development of the Valiram corporate website.
- Built custom plugins, reusable theme components, and dynamic page templates.
- Developed brand-management features using custom post types, taxonomies, tags, and structured custom fields.
- Implemented dynamic store directories with country, store type and brand filtering.
- Created custom job-listing layouts, filters, taxonomies, and brand-related career sections.
- Built reusable carousel, timeline, lookbook, reels, and content-display components.
- Designed and implemented a custom AJAX search solution to deliver faster, more relevant search results across multiple content types.
- Improved the search interface with dynamic result loading, keyword handling, and a smoother user experience.
- Integrated responsive frontend behaviour using PHP, JavaScript, jQuery, Bootstrap, and WordPress APIs.
- Worked closely with design, content, QA, and business teams to deliver enhancements and resolve production issues.
- Maintained the platform through ongoing feature development and optimisation.

## Outcome

The result is a scalable and content-driven corporate platform that allows Valiram to manage multiple brands, markets, stores, careers, campaigns, and editorial content within a unified WordPress environment.

The reusable architecture reduced the need for repeated page development, improved consistency across the website, and made future content and feature updates easier to manage as business requirements evolved.

The custom AJAX search also improved content discovery by allowing visitors to find relevant information more quickly through a faster, smoother, and more responsive search experience.
