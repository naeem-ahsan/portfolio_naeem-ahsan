## Overview

PROLAB is a Malaysia-based product development and marketing agency working across brand identity, marketing materials, visual merchandising and production. The website brings these connected capabilities into one focused digital platform while introducing the agency’s positioning, services and portfolio.

The project was delivered as a custom WordPress implementation, giving PROLAB a distinctive frontend experience alongside a practical content-management workflow for its internal team.

## Challenge

PROLAB’s offering spans strategy, creative development, product development and physical production. The website needed to communicate that breadth without making the visitor journey feel fragmented or overwhelming.

It also needed to preserve the brand’s bold visual identity across desktop, tablet and mobile while remaining accessible, responsive and straightforward to maintain after launch.

## Approach

- Built a custom WordPress theme around reusable, modular PHP components.
- Created structured content workflows for services, projects, partners and frequently asked questions.
- Used ACF and native WordPress controls to make key website content manageable without editing code.
- Translated PROLAB’s visual direction into responsive layouts, interactive service previews and portfolio experiences.
- Added GSAP-powered transitions and Lenis momentum scrolling with reduced-motion support.
- Used Vite and SCSS to organise, compile and optimise the frontend assets.
- Applied responsive image handling, WebP conversion and performance-conscious loading practices.
- Added production-focused security controls and a customised administration experience.

## Contributions

- Developed and launched the website as a custom WordPress implementation.
- Built reusable theme components and responsive layouts for the homepage, service pages, project pages, project archive, FAQ, contact and error pages.
- Developed custom post types and taxonomies for services, projects, partners and FAQs.
- Implemented CMS-managed service previews, project galleries, portfolio content and partner listings.
- Built responsive desktop and mobile navigation and service interactions.
- Added smooth scrolling, viewport-triggered motion and milestone counter animations.
- Implemented native project duplication and media-management enhancements for editors.
- Added production security hardening, custom login styling and administrative restrictions.
- Tested and refined the experience across common screen sizes before deployment.
- Prepared client-facing documentation for ongoing content management and website maintenance.

## Outcome

The completed website gives PROLAB a focused public platform for presenting its integrated creative, marketing and production services.

Visitors can understand the agency’s capabilities, explore its portfolio and move from discovery to enquiry through a consistent responsive experience. PROLAB’s team can manage services, projects, partners and other core content through WordPress without relying on a developer for routine updates.
